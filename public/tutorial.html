How this is graded

The rubric is fixed

The criteria, the levels and the weights come from the course, not from the model. The AI cannot add or soften a criterion.

The AI drafts, and must quote

For every criterion it proposes a level and quotes the passage of your paper it relied on. No quotation, no level.

The instructor decides

The instructor reads each quotation against your paper, changes any level they disagree with, and only then confirms the grade.

You see the evidence too

The returned grade shows the same quotations, so you can see what the level was awarded on — and argue with it.
