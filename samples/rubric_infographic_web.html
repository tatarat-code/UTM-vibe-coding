Corporate In-house Startup Programs: A Management Assessment

Assignment rubric · 3 learning objectives × 3 levels · 1,200–1,500 words · ≥ 5 sources (APA 7) · AI drafts the grade with quoted evidence, the instructor decides
100points total
LO1 · CLO1
Positioning
30WEIGHT
Positions the program accurately within technology strategy and the technology acquisition approaches.
3Excellent
  • Defines the program and distinguishes its types (call-for-proposals, CVC-linked, spin-off)
  • States its purpose within technology strategy
  • Explains, with literature, how it differs from and complements in-house R&D, alliances, industry–government–academia and external venture collaboration
2Satisfactory
  • Definition and strategic purpose are stated
  • Comparison with other acquisition approaches is partial, or program types are unclear
1Developing
  • General description only
  • Link to technology strategy and acquisition approaches is missing or incorrect
LO2 · CLO2
Multi-perspective analysis
40WEIGHT
Analyses merits and demerits from the perspective of corporate management, on the basis of evidence.
3Excellent
  • Both merits and demerits, from ≥ 3 perspectives (resource allocation, R&D organisation, people & careers, IP & governance, finance, portfolio)
  • Every argument backed by literature or a concrete case
  • Trade-offs addressed (e.g., autonomy vs control)
2Satisfactory
  • Both sides covered, but ≤ 2 perspectives or some arguments unsupported
  • Trade-offs only weakly mentioned
1Developing
  • One-sided (merits or demerits only)
  • No evidence; a list of general statements or opinions
LO3 · CLO3
Recommendations & academic writing
30WEIGHT
Derives feasible recommendations from the analysis and presents them in an appropriate academic form.
3Excellent
  • Recommendations follow logically from LO2; target company's conditions specified
  • Concrete measures: evaluation criteria, resource rules, interface with parent, exit criteria
  • Clear structure · ≥ 5 sources in correct APA · word count met
2Satisfactory
  • Recommendations only partly tied to the analysis, or generic measures
  • Minor deficiencies in structure or citation
1Developing
  • No recommendations, or unrelated to the analysis
  • Unclear structure; citations missing; word count far off
Scoring
total = Σ ( weight × level ÷ 3 )
Example: LO1 = 3, LO2 = 2, LO3 = 3 → 30 + 26.7 + 30 = 86.7
Level rule
A level is awarded only when all its conditions are met. If any condition of a higher level is missing, award the level below. LO3 formal requirements are checked independently of content.
Evidence rule
Every level descriptor can be verified by a quoted passage from the submission. The AI drafts the grade with evidence; the instructor examines it and releases the final grade.
